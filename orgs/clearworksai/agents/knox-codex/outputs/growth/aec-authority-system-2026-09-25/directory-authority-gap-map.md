## Avoid or defer

- AEC Hub tool directory: Clearworks is currently a consultancy, not a software tool.
- AEC Magazine AI Spotlight: product-specific submission; not a fit without a public standalone product.
- AI in AEC tool hub: app/tool database and no public submission path; do not force the consulting practice into it.
- AIA architect-firm directories and ACEC firm directory: entity/licensure mismatch.
- Pay-to-rank general agency directories: defer until analytics shows meaningful generic consulting demand.
- Local chambers: consider only for genuine network value and consistent location evidence, not backlink acquisition alone.

## Reusable evidence kit

Prepare once, then adapt without changing the underlying facts:

1. 40-word and 100-word Clearworks descriptions.
2. Service taxonomy: workflow audit, AI implementation, workflow automation, training/adoption, connected systems, and operations visibility.
3. Josh bio with substantiated credentials only.
4. AIA|LA Office Hours and TAP evidence.
5. Opportunity records clearly labeled as opportunities rather than realized savings.
6. Permissioned implementation proof and one or two verified client reviews.
7. Owned logo/headshot/event imagery with a source and permission ledger.
8. Canonical URLs and consistent Los Angeles business/location/contact data.

## Strategic role

Directories are distribution and corroboration, not the intellectual product. Qualified consultant/company/expert listings should point to Clearworks' original research, book companion data, practical AEC decision guides, and implementation evidence. The book and public evidence layer supply the depth; the directories make the entity easier to discover and trust.
