Date: 2026-09-28

Status: internal decision matrix; current commitments separated from unresolved terms; **not approved page copy**

## Decision

The canonical offer-ladder artifacts are coherent, but current public surfaces still contain naming and routing inconsistencies. `/starter` cannot yet describe the first implementation engagement as a complete product contract. The page accurately explains the work and boundary; it does not yet state the buyer's inputs, acceptance, scope-change breakpoint, handoff, exit, or ongoing commercial terms with enough precision.

## Recommended page-level wording boundary

The page may explain:

- what Clearworks builds and maintains;
- what the client must contribute;
- how a candidate becomes an accepted record;
- what triggers specialist involvement or a scope decision;
- what a clean handoff contains;
- that the buyer can stop, continue, or choose the next priority at a defined decision point.

The page should not publish until approved:

- a universal implementation price;
- a fixed duration or cycle count for every workflow;
- a guarantee of savings, adoption, accuracy, or ranking;
- a minimum term, cancellation policy, transition obligation, or hosting/support promise not present in the signed commercial model;
- a direct-sprint path that contradicts the current “Audit first” page.
