## 4. Program scope

### 4.1 Existing-skill staged improvements

The following four staged bundles remain eligible for independent review:

1. `audit-pipeline`: source-to-outcome trace IDs, confidence, negative evidence,
   alternatives, coverage, and proportionality gates.
2. `source-collection`: primary-source preference, claim-level source binding,
   query/retrieval receipts, event-vs-publication dates, conflicts, confidence,
   link verification, and coverage summaries.
3. `morning-review`: delta-vs-carry-forward separation, receipt-bound claims,
   source reconciliation, ownership/lifecycle/next-trigger classification, and
   concise consequential delivery with durable links.
4. `audit-assemble-master`: section and citation coverage, rendered-page QA,
   delivered file/link verification, stable hashes, and terminal acceptance.

No staged bundle becomes live during this program's planning phase.

### 4.3 Cross-cutting doctrine

The reusable principles are maintained separately from skill-specific diffs:

- source authority and claim-level provenance;
- explicit uncertainty, conflicts, negative evidence, and unresolved states;
- output verification through render, package, link, hash, and receipt checks;
- confidentiality partitioning at storage and delivery boundaries;
- lifecycle-aware ownership and idempotent reconciliation;
- staged full-bundle delivery, reversibility, and no silent live overwrite;
- retired-product artifacts remain forensic only; reusable rules migrate into
  product-neutral successors.

## 5. Confidentiality classes

- `open_source`: generalized methodology without client/user/project details.
- `internal`: Clearworks operating preferences and fleet mechanics.
- `client_confidential`: client identities, evidence, relationship/deal state,
  deliverables, or private source material.
- `personal_confidential`: Josh's email, calendar, finances, meetings, and
  personal commitments.
- `public_ready`: content that separately passed evidence, voice, rights, and
  delivery approval.

Raw evidence never enters generalized staged bundles. Reviews preserve the
partition of the source observation and publish only generalized principles.

## 7. Execution gates

### G0 — Evidence freeze

Verify the review, manifest, five archive hashes, Task Observer source
attribution, partition permissions, and correction source. Any mismatch stops.

### G1 — Correction binding

The correction ledger and manifest must exclude the legacy archive from every
install/apply plan while preserving its bytes and hash as forensic evidence.

### G2 — Existing-skill review

Independently inspect the four eligible full bundles against fresh live skill
sources. Rebase any stale bundle, rerun reference/package/confidentiality gates,
and return accept/correct decisions. No installation.

### G3 — Cross-cutting doctrine review

Approve, correct, or reject each generalized principle independently. Do not
propagate doctrine into skills before this gate passes.

### G4 — New-skill contract review

Specify each candidate's purpose, inputs, outputs, enforcement, confidentiality,
licensing/distribution, and pre-flight verification. Reject overlap before any
new skill is authored.

### G5 — Staged authoring

After G2-G4 acceptance, author only approved changes as complete staged bundles
with manifests and byte-derived diffs. Installed skill paths remain untouched.

### G6 — Independent acceptance

Require exact path equality, reference completeness, archive listing, privacy
sweeps, reversibility proof, and zero legacy-product activation. Findings of
HIGH or CRITICAL block installation.

### G7 — Install decision

Installation or fleet activation requires a separate explicit decision. It is
not authorized by this specification or its Goalify hierarchy.

## 8. Non-goals

- No ClearPath or Academy improvement, reactivation, routing, or deployment.
- No silent overwrite of an installed skill.
- No installation, fleet activation, daemon restart, merge, push, or deploy.
- No copying raw client/personal evidence into shared or open-source artifacts.
- No treating a new-skill candidate as approved merely because it appears here.

## 9. Acceptance condition

The planning program is complete when G0 and G1 pass, the Goalify hierarchy is
immutable and internally consistent, all proposed work is classified by gate,
the legacy bundle is visibly excluded everywhere, and a reviewer can reproduce
the hashes and scope without relying on conversation history.
